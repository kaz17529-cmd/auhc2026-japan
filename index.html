As per the official tournament rules, each team must supply one qualified and experienced referee (Level 2 recommended). The cost of not providing a team referee is EUR 1,500 per team. Details of the Team Referee must be submitted by May 31, 2026, and the fee is due by June 30, 2026. 💡 審判（Referee）不帯同ペナルティ費用（確定）：
